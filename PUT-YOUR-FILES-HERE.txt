Put your images and sounds directly in this folder (public/), using
these exact file names (they are the defaults the component loads):

IMAGES
  background.jpg       scrolling pixel background on the search screen
  battle-bg.jpg        static striped background once a Pokemon is found
  grass-top.png        grass patch under the wild Pokemon
  grass-bottom.png     grass patch under your Pokemon
  mightyenaback.png    your Pokemon's back sprite
  trainer.gif          animated trainer on the search screen
  trainerstill.jpg     still frame of the trainer

SOUNDS
  MightyenaCry.mp3     your Pokemon's cry
  BattleMusic.mp3      battle music intro
  BattleMusicLoop.mp3  battle music loop
  ConfirmSound.mp3     select / confirm sound
  Escape.mp3           back / escape sound

You can delete this text file afterwards. To use different names or
folders, pass them via the `assets` prop, e.g.
  <PokeSearch assets={{ trainer: '/img/trainer.gif' }} />
